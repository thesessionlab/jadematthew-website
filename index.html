I direct audio drama and voice sessions, and cast for games, commercials, music videos and dubbing.

About

On screen, behind the camera and in the studio.

I'm an actor, voice artist and director. I've worked across television, film, radio, voiceover, theatre, video games and content for clients in the UK and abroad.

Because I've worked on both sides of the camera and the studio glass, I know what you need from a session, and I'll stay with you from the first brief to the final take.

Outside work I love music, food and breathwork. My family goes back to Victorian butchers in Tottenham.

I also coach performers and leaders through Session.
